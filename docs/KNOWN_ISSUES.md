## Issue Template

When adding a new issue, use this format:

```
## ISS-XXX: Short Title

**Severity**: CRITICAL | HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW
**Status**: OPEN | IN PROGRESS | BLOCKED | VERIFIED FIXED | WONT FIX
**Component**: [e.g., Editor, Sync, Export, Auth, Analytics]
**Date Opened**: YYYY-MM-DD

### Reproduction Steps
1. Step one
2. Step two
3. Step three

### Expected Behavior
What should happen.

### Actual Behavior
What actually happens.

### Root Cause (if known)
Analysis of why this occurs.

### Next Action
What needs to be done to resolve or investigate further.

### Related Tasks/Commits
Links to implementation tasks, PRs, or commits.
```
